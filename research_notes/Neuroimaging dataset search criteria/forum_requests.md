# What people ask for when requesting neuroimaging datasets in public forums

Method note: NeuroStars (and the MNE forum and AFNI message board, both Discourse) were searched through the Discourse JSON search API with about 80 query variants ("looking for dataset", "publicly available EEG", "dataset Alzheimer", "7T dataset", ...). Opening posts were filtered for request phrasing, and the full raw text was pulled from `https://neurostars.org/raw/<id>/1`. Stack Exchange (Psychology & Neuroscience, Open Data, Cross Validated, Data Science) was searched through api.stackexchange.com. GitHub was searched with `gh search issues` (meagmohit/EEG-Datasets, OpenNeuroOrg/openneuro). Every quote below was copied from the retrieved post text, including the original typos. Dates are the creation dates of the opening posts.

**Access limits:** Reddit was not reachable. WebFetch reports "unable to fetch from www.reddit.com", other Reddit endpoints were refused in this environment, and web search returned no Reddit threads. There is therefore **no Reddit data** here. Twitter/Mastodon and the FSL/SPM (JISCmail) and EEGLAB mailing lists were not searched because of the time and tool budget. The AFNI board returned no dataset-request threads for the queries used.

## Q1. Corpus of real, verbatim dataset requests with coded criteria

### Takeaway
I collected 43 genuine "find me a dataset" requests from 2013 to 2025 (NeuroStars 24, Stack Exchange 15, MNE forum 1, GitHub 3). I also collected 12 "meta" requests: people asking for search or filter capabilities over dataset metadata, mostly NeuroStars and OpenNeuro GitHub issues. Requests are short. Most are **modality + diagnosis/population** or **modality + task/rest**. A sizeable minority ask for preprocessed or derivative data. Very few mention age, sex, site, or sample size.

Codes: Sp = species; Dx = population/diagnosis (including "healthy"); Age; Sex; Mod = modality; Task = task/paradigm (including resting state); N = sample size; Site; Ses = longitudinal/sessions; Der = derivatives/preprocessed; Scan = scanner/field strength/sequence; MM = multiple modalities or scanners in the same subjects; Acc = open/public/licence; Fmt = data format; Oth = other.

### Cited Findings
- Requesters often report that they had already searched OpenNeuro or other portals and failed. Examples: "I already searched through OpenNeuro and don't find anymore" ([N7](https://neurostars.org/t/inter-subject-decoding-datasets/5965)); "I checked the openneuro.org website, as well as the fcon_1000 projects, but no success" ([N3](https://neurostars.org/t/looking-for-open-fmri-datasets-alcohol-consumptions/3703)); "I'm not sure if I'm searching improperly" ([N22](https://neurostars.org/t/looking-for-test-fmri-data-with-dir-ap-and-dir-pa-scans/29465)); "I've been searching forever and haven't even come close" ([S4](https://psychology.stackexchange.com/questions/19500/task-based-fmri-dataset-with-very-simple-task-e-g-view-stripes)).
- Requesters often anchor on a dataset they already know and ask for "more like this": "similar to them fmri datasets like abide" ([N2](https://neurostars.org/t/eeg-datasets-for-autism-control/1372)); "I'm currently using HCP data" ([N16](https://neurostars.org/t/language-fmri-task-public-datasets/22405)); a list of known datasets (Kay/Gallant, BOLD5000, GenericObjectDecoding) ([N12](https://neurostars.org/t/looking-for-public-fmri-datasets-viewing-natural-images/13543)); "i know of the openneuro forrest dataset" ([N8](https://neurostars.org/t/open-datasets-with-tof-swi-images/6640)); "For now the one I'm mostly using are Individual Brain Charting ; BOLD5000 ; Forrest" ([N7](https://neurostars.org/t/inter-subject-decoding-datasets/5965)).
- Requesters sometimes state exclusions as well as inclusions: "no RS" ([N7](https://neurostars.org/t/inter-subject-decoding-datasets/5965)); "MP-RAGE (not MP2-RAGE)" ([S2](https://psychology.stackexchange.com/questions/12988/open-access-7t-resting-state-fmri-dataset)); "HCP, MSC (Midnight Scanning Club) don't have proper decoding tasks" ([N7](https://neurostars.org/t/inter-subject-decoding-datasets/5965)).
- A maintainer of OpenNeuro wrote that exact keyword search does not "correspond to how people actually search," citing EEG vs sEEG/iEEG and M/EEG ([OpenNeuro #2586](https://github.com/OpenNeuroOrg/openneuro/issues/2586)).

### Inferences
- The typical request is two or three constraints long: modality + (diagnosis | rest/task) + "open/preprocessed". Long multi-constraint specifications (N7, N22, S4) come from methods developers.
- Many requesters are students or ML practitioners (N17, S5, S11, S13, N19). They ask for "preprocessed", "labelled", or image-format data and treat diagnosis as a class label.

### Gaps
- No Reddit, Twitter/Mastodon, FSL/SPM JISCmail, or EEGLAB-list requests were collected (blocked or not searched). The corpus is therefore skewed towards NeuroStars (BIDS/OpenNeuro-literate users) and Stack Exchange.
- Answers and replies were not systematically read, so I did not record which requests were ever satisfied.

## Q2. Frequency tally of criteria (main corpus A, n = 43)

### Takeaway
Modality is near-universal. After that the most frequent criteria are explicit openness/public access, diagnosis/population, task/paradigm (with resting state the single most common "task"), and derivatives/preprocessed. Demographics (age, sex), site/country, and longitudinal design are almost never requested in free-text forum posts, even though they are standard search facets.

Modality mix among the 41: fMRI 20 (including resting-state fMRI and N15's list); EEG 11; structural/other MRI (T1/MP-RAGE, TOF, SWI, pCASL, FLAIR, tumour MRI, low-field) 9; DWI/DTI 2; fNIRS 2 (N15, N23); CT 1; MEG 0; PET 0; iEEG 0. Some requests name more than one modality.

Meta corpus B (n = 12): the criteria people want as *search filters* are subject age/sex ([X3](https://neurostars.org/t/datalad-search-openneuro/18386), [X11](https://github.com/OpenNeuroOrg/openneuro/issues/2921)), sessions ([X9](https://github.com/OpenNeuroOrg/openneuro/issues/2775)), derivatives with pipeline version ([X10](https://github.com/OpenNeuroOrg/openneuro/issues/2811)), file names/suffixes/sidecar keys ([X1](https://neurostars.org/t/intro-to-datalad-searching-through-file-jsons-sidecars/4709), [X6](https://github.com/OpenNeuroOrg/openneuro/issues/2568), [X7](https://github.com/OpenNeuroOrg/openneuro/issues/3983)), species ([X12](https://github.com/OpenNeuroOrg/openneuro/issues/3904)), modality hierarchy ([X8](https://github.com/OpenNeuroOrg/openneuro/issues/2586)), manufacturer ([X3](https://neurostars.org/t/datalad-search-openneuro/18386)), and dataset size/duration/licence/brain region ([X5](https://neurostars.org/t/is-there-any-compilation-of-the-metadata-from-most-different-neuro-data-repositories/27562)).

### Inferences
- Free-text requests and filter-style requests differ. Free-text askers lead with modality, condition, and task. Power users who want filters ask for subject-level demographics, sessions, file/suffix presence, and acquisition parameters. A natural-language engine needs both: semantic matching on condition and task, and structured filtering on N, age, sex, sessions, and suffix.
- "Open/public" appears in half of the requests, but it is mostly a default assumption, not a discriminating filter. Licence is a real constraint only for redistribution or commercial ML use (S9, S15).
- "Resting state" behaves like a modality-level term (rs-fMRI, "resting-state EEG"). It should be recognised as a first-class concept, not just as a task name.

### Gaps
- n = 43, coded by a single coder, so the percentages are indicative only. No inter-rater reliability was checked.
- MEG, PET, and iEEG never appeared in the direct-request corpus. This probably reflects the sources sampled (no MNE-list or Reddit coverage), not an absence of demand.

## Q3. Criteria that would be hard to answer from metadata

### Takeaway
About a third of the requests contain at least one constraint that is not in BIDS metadata (participants.tsv, dataset_description.json, sidecars). These are mostly stimulus content, design structure, data quality/quantity, pairing of the same subjects across scanners, and the availability of particular phenotype variables or labels.

### Cited Findings
- **Stimulus content / naturalistic:** "viewing natural images (maybe preferably single object)" ([N12](https://neurostars.org/t/looking-for-public-fmri-datasets-viewing-natural-images/13543)); "videos of people talking ... The video should include the face of the speaker" ([N18](https://neurostars.org/t/fmri-dataset-of-people-watching-others-talking/28089)); "very simple visual tasks, like viewing vertical and horizontal stripes" ([S4](https://psychology.stackexchange.com/questions/19500/task-based-fmri-dataset-with-very-simple-task-e-g-view-stripes)); "EEG signal and its image pair collected with different scenes" ([G1](https://github.com/meagmohit/EEG-Datasets/issues/20)).
- **Design structure:** "non-trivial decoding task. (block-design)" ([N7](https://neurostars.org/t/inter-subject-decoding-datasets/5965)); ""rest-task-rest" design (scanned in one day)" ([N11](https://neurostars.org/t/cant-find-an-open-fmri-dataset-with-rest-task-rest-design/7207)); "at least 20 Participants and 30 trials per task. It should also be randomized" ([S4](https://psychology.stackexchange.com/questions/19500/task-based-fmri-dataset-with-very-simple-task-e-g-view-stripes)).
- **Amount of data:** "Longer the duration the better" ([N18](https://neurostars.org/t/fmri-dataset-of-people-watching-others-talking/28089)). X5 wanted "dataset size" and "duration" because existing CSVs "crucially don't include dataset size" ([NeuroStars](https://neurostars.org/t/is-there-any-compilation-of-the-metadata-from-most-different-neuro-data-repositories/27562)).
- **Same subjects on different scanners or modalities (paired):** "low-field MRI ... paired to something at least 3T" ([N20](https://neurostars.org/t/dataset-lowfield-mri/28588)); "MR-CT Paired images" ([S15](https://opendata.stackexchange.com/questions/20927/mri-ct-paired-open-dataset-for-mri-synthesis-from-ct-using-supervised-learning)); "both task fMRI langauge and DTI data" ([N16](https://neurostars.org/t/language-fmri-task-public-datasets/22405)). Partly answerable when both modalities sit in one BIDS dataset, but not across datasets.
- **Acquisition parameters buried in sidecars:** "fast TR, ideally lower than 1.0 and ideally a range of TRs" ([N5](https://neurostars.org/t/fast-tr-open-task-fmri-datasets/5601)); "MP-RAGE (not MP2-RAGE)" ([S2](https://psychology.stackexchange.com/questions/12988/open-access-7t-resting-state-fmri-dataset)); "dir-AP and dir-PA" ([N22](https://neurostars.org/t/looking-for-test-fmri-data-with-dir-ap-and-dir-pa-scans/29465)); "EffectiveEchoSpacing" ([X1](https://neurostars.org/t/intro-to-datalad-searching-through-file-jsons-sidecars/4709)). Answerable only if sidecar JSON and file names are indexed. Sequence names such as MP-RAGE vs MP2-RAGE are often absent or free text.
- **Specific phenotype variables:** "alcohol dependence questionnaires (AUDIT, ADS, etc....)" ([N3](https://neurostars.org/t/looking-for-open-fmri-datasets-alcohol-consumptions/3703)); "tagged with phenotype or disease" ([S6](https://psychology.stackexchange.com/questions/25829/brain-network-datasets)). These require indexing the phenotype/ directory or the README.
- **Auxiliary recordings:** "pcasl dataset with physiological recording (rv/hr)" ([N8](https://neurostars.org/t/open-datasets-with-tof-swi-images/6640)); "EEG data + Digitization" ([M1](https://mne.discourse.group/t/eeg-digitization-usecase/1872)). Detectable from the presence of `_physio`/`_electrodes`/`_coordsystem` files, but only with file-level indexing.
- **Labels / ground truth / ML-readiness:** "I want a labeled dataset" ([S13](https://opendata.stackexchange.com/questions/18515/can-i-find-a-dataset-of-eeg-signals-to-classify-a-depression)); "ground truth images" ([S12](https://opendata.stackexchange.com/questions/17904/free-grayscale-images-dataset-with-ground-truth-to-pre-train-u-net-used-later-in)); tumour sub-type classes ([S11](https://opendata.stackexchange.com/questions/17703/3d-brain-tumor-datasets-for-classification)); "preprocessed ... for use in machine learning tasks" ([N17](https://neurostars.org/t/need-preprocessed-fmri-dataset/23131)).
- **Specific preprocessing state:** "preprocessed using fMRIPrep" ([N10](https://neurostars.org/t/open-source-datasets/7155)); "brain extracted ... PNG/JPG/JPEG" ([N19](https://neurostars.org/t/need-preprocessed-dataset-of-schizophrenia/28441)); "functional connectivity networks ... .graphml or just adjacency matrices" ([S7](https://psychology.stackexchange.com/questions/26095/connectome-datasets-related-to-consciousness)); "preprocessed (in a standard manner)" ([S1](https://psychology.stackexchange.com/questions/3889/open-fmri-data)).
- **Brain states / constructs:** "subjects emotional state" ([S5](https://psychology.stackexchange.com/questions/20526/fmri-datasets-for-emotion-states)); "consciousness research such as DoC , sleep, or possibly on mindfulness meditation" ([S7](https://psychology.stackexchange.com/questions/26095/connectome-datasets-related-to-consciousness)); "recording the visual cortex" ([N12](https://neurostars.org/t/looking-for-public-fmri-datasets-viewing-natural-images/13543)), which is a brain region or field-of-view constraint.
- **Similarity to a named dataset:** "similar to them fmri datasets like abide" ([N2](https://neurostars.org/t/eeg-datasets-for-autism-control/1372)).
- **Vocabulary mismatch inside metadata:** sex coded as "M"/"F" vs "Male"/"Female" ([OpenNeuro #2921](https://github.com/OpenNeuroOrg/openneuro/issues/2921)); EEG vs sEEG/iEEG ([#2586](https://github.com/OpenNeuroOrg/openneuro/issues/2586)); title word "spinal" not found ([X2](https://neurostars.org/t/dysfunctional-search-feature-in-openneuro-website/4983)).

### Inferences
- Many "hard" criteria could be partly answered if the engine indexes file names, BIDS suffixes, sidecar keys (RepetitionTime, PhaseEncodingDirection, MagneticFieldStrength, PulseSequenceType), events.tsv trial counts, the phenotype/ directory, and README text. Stimulus *content* (faces, natural images, talking) depends almost entirely on README/paper text, which suits LLM-generated summaries or semantic search.
- "Similar to dataset X" queries suggest supporting dataset-to-dataset similarity ("more like ds000xyz").

### Gaps
- I did not check how many of these criteria are actually populated across OpenNeuro datasets. That needs a separate metadata-coverage analysis.

## Q4. How curated lists and portals categorise datasets

### Takeaway
Community lists categorise mainly by **paradigm/application** (EEG lists) or by **organism / level / data type / disorder / access** (general neuroscience lists). OpenNeuro's own faceted search uses modality, age range, participant count, diagnosis, task, author, sex, date, species, study type (cross-sectional/longitudinal), study domain, body part, scanner manufacturer/model, PET tracer, and raw vs derivative.

### Cited Findings
- **meagmohit/EEG-Datasets** groups datasets by paradigm or application: "Motor-Imagery" (~11), "Emotion-Recognition" (~10), "Error-Related Potentials (ErrP)", "Visually Evoked Potentials (VEPs)", "Event Related Potentials [ERPs]" (~12), "Slow-Cortical Potentials (SCPs)", "Resting State", "Music and EEG", "Eye-blinks/movements" (~9), "Miscellaneous", "Clinical EEG" (2), plus unfiltered "Others" and "Resources to search for database". Per-dataset attributes are not standardised, but most give number of subjects, number of channels/device, and sometimes sampling rate, trials/sessions, and task ([GitHub](https://github.com/meagmohit/EEG-Datasets)). Counts are approximate, from a WebFetch summary of the README.
- **Wikipedia "List of neuroscience databases"** columns: "Name", "Description", "Organism", "Level (gene, neuron, macroscopic)", "Data (MRI, fMRI, images, descriptive, numerical)", "Disorder", "Register to view data?", "Ref." ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_neuroscience_databases)).
- **OpenNeuro search facets** (source code `initial-search-params.tsx`): keywords; modality (MRI, PET, EEG, iEEG, MEG, NIRS, with commented-out MRI sub-types "Functional", "Structural", "Diffusion", "ASL Perfusion" and PET "Static"/"Dynamic"); ageRange; subjectCountRange; diagnosis ("Healthy / Control", "Schizophrenia", "ADD/ADHD", "Alzheimers", "Other"); tasks; authors; sex ("Male", "Female"); date published; species ("Human", "Rat", "Mouse", "Other"); section ("Cross-Sectional", "Longitudinal", "Other"); studyDomains; bodyParts; scannerManufacturers; scannerManufacturersModelNames; tracerNames; tracerRadionuclides; bidsDatasetType ("raw", "derivative"); brain_initiative; syntheticDataset ([GitHub OpenNeuro source](https://github.com/OpenNeuroOrg/openneuro/blob/master/packages/openneuro-app/src/scripts/search/initial-search-params.tsx)). Open feature requests add NHP to species ([#3904](https://github.com/OpenNeuroOrg/openneuro/issues/3904)), number of sessions ([#2775](https://github.com/OpenNeuroOrg/openneuro/issues/2775)), suffix filter such as FLAIR ([#3504](https://github.com/OpenNeuroOrg/openneuro/issues/3504)), NIRS and Motion modalities ([#3094](https://github.com/OpenNeuroOrg/openneuro/issues/3094)), and derivative-specific fields such as fMRIPrep/MRIQC version ([#2811](https://github.com/OpenNeuroOrg/openneuro/issues/2811)).
- **NeuroDataHub** (announced on NeuroStars, 2025) describes each of 50+ datasets with "modality types, sample sizes, age ranges, scanner types, and direct links for data download and documentation". It emphasises "diverse populations, age groups, and scanner types" and modalities "structural MRI (T1-weighted) ... fMRI ... DWI ... PET, cognitive tests, behavioral assessments, and fluid biomarkers" ([NeuroStars](https://neurostars.org/t/neurodatahub-explore-compare-50-open-neuroimaging-datasets-lifespan-multimodal/33504)).
- A NeuroStars user designing a cross-repository metadata sheet chose: "modality, dataset size, organism, n_subjects, ages, condition(s), duration, brain region, license, authors, paper" ([NeuroStars](https://neurostars.org/t/is-there-any-compilation-of-the-metadata-from-most-different-neuro-data-repositories/27562)).
- A 2020 NeuroStars post compiling public datasets for student projects (apparently in a summer-school context, not verified) points students to other lists: openlists/ElectrophysiologyData, NeuroTechX awesome-bci "brain-databases", TUH EEG, and Neurotycho. It warns that "these vary in their quality of metadata & organization" ([NeuroStars](https://neurostars.org/t/a-collection-of-publicly-available-datasets/14293)).
- OpenNeuro links to Neurobagel as an "Annotated search" for cohort-level queries ([#2956](https://github.com/OpenNeuroOrg/openneuro/issues/2956)). A Neurobagel GSoC description says it builds tools "to define and find cohorts of individuals across a federated ecosystem of data nodes" ([NeuroStars](https://neurostars.org/t/gsoc-2024-project-idea-19-1-an-llm-assisted-service-for-annotating-research-data-with-machine-understandable-semantic-data-dictionaries-175-350-h/28279)).

### Inferences
- The curated lists show two different organising axes. EEG/BCI communities think in **paradigm** (motor imagery, ERP, emotion), which matches the task-centric forum requests. Clinical/MRI communities think in **population x modality x demographics** (NeuroDataHub, OpenNeuro facets). The forum corpus shows **paradigm/stimulus** and **preprocessing state** as frequent request axes. OpenNeuro's facets cover these only through free-text "tasks" and the raw/derivative flag.
- OpenNeuro's diagnosis facet has only 4 named categories. Forum diagnoses (autism, epilepsy, tumour, bipolar, depression, Covid-19, disorders of consciousness) mostly fall into "Other", which argues for free-text or ontology-based condition matching.

### Gaps
- openlists/ElectrophysiologyData, NeuroTechX awesome-bci, and OpenNeuro's curated collections or portal pages were not fetched, so their categories are not recorded here.
- The rendered OpenNeuro UI was not inspected. The facets are taken from source code, so which ones are visible to users or currently populated was not verified.
