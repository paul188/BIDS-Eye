Is this a term? If yes, a search for it should match its words only when they stand together.

Written as the phrase

Words in the text, but not together

Keys: 1–4 label and advance, ← →, N note. Sorted by the number of datasets that contain all the words.

What the options mean

Term: the words together name one thing (a task, construct, condition, region, method or acquisition setting). A search for it should require the words close together.
Boilerplate: README housekeeping (licences, consent, ethics, curator notes, file layout). Nobody searches for it; its words should not make a dataset match a search for something else.
Not a term: words that often stand together but name nothing (names, "took place", URL pieces).
Unsure: can't tell from the phrase and the examples.

Counts: as phrase = datasets that write the words together (space, hyphen or as one word; never across a full stop or comma); words anywhere = datasets that contain all the words; short fields = datasets whose name, description, study fields or LLM-read conditions have the phrase.
