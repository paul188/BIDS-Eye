Should a search for find datasets that only say ?

Forms separated by “/” are plural and singular of one word; judge them as one.

Keys: 1–5 label and advance, ← →, N note
What the options mean

Both directions: the two mean the same here; each search should find the other.
Only A → B: a search for A should also find B, but a search for B should not find A.
Only B → A: the reverse.
Neither: different meanings; neither search should find the other.
Depends on context: sometimes the same meaning, sometimes not; the word alone can't decide.
